What you can claim for

Landlords have a legal duty to keep the structure and exterior of your home, and its heating, hot water and sanitation, in good repair, and to keep it fit to live in. If they fail after being told, you may be able to claim.

The amount depends on how bad the problem was, how long it lasted and how it affected you. We cannot promise an outcome, but we will tell you honestly what your claim looks like.

The repairs themselves
A court or settlement can require your landlord to fix the defects within a set time.
Compensation for the disruption
For months or years of living with damp, cold, noise from works or loss of use of a room.
Damaged belongings
Furniture, clothing, bedding and flooring ruined by water or mould.
Extra costs
Higher heating bills, laundry, cleaning and anything else the problem cost you.
